KeepT privacy notice

KeepT reads and translates the text on your screen entirely on your computer. Screenshots,
recognized text and translations never leave your PC. There are no accounts and no telemetry.

KeepT only connects to the internet for two things:

1. Downloading translation models, the first time a language pair is needed. They come from
   the KeepT release on GitHub (github.com/carranza-javier/KeepT, release "models-v1") or, if
   GitHub cannot be reached, from the official OPUS-MT servers of the University of Helsinki
   (object.pouta.csc.fi). Only the model file is requested.

2. Checking for new KeepT versions, at most once a day, by asking the public GitHub API for the
   newest release. No information about you or your screen is sent. You can turn this off on
   the next page of this installer or later in the tray menu ("Check for updates").

GitHub and the University of Helsinki servers may log the requests they receive (for example
your IP address), as described in their own privacy policies.
